You can take the quiz as many times as you like, with no time limit. Your highest score counts.

- Which locators are robust, and why scoping a locator matters on the Canvas login page.
- Why `expect()` replaces `sleep()`.
- What `storage_state` saves, and why it stays out of git.
- What a browser-use agent is good for, and what it should never be.
